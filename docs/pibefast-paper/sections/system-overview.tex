\section{System Overview}
\label{sec:system}
The embodied AI workflow couples physical sensing, prompted user actions, renewed acquisition, and feedback. The monitoring tier seeks candidate warning signs; guided checks acquire task-specific evidence. The Pi tier continuously observes balance and speech at reduced rates.
A retained trigger requests an active check on the local computer, where the
user selects or follows B/E/F/A/S tasks. A persistent unresolved-event queue
supports delivery retries and acknowledgement. During guided interaction,
passive processing is paused as appropriate; the workflow resumes monitoring
after the active session.

Figure~\ref{fig:overview} summarizes the audio/video sensing and feedback workflow. Medical-record processing is implemented externally; its interface and performance are outside this evaluation. The sensing service produces component measurements, acquisition-quality reasons, a research feature vector, and an independent urgency/completeness assessment. Eligible measurements and symptom reports contribute directly to the urgency rule.

Each component record includes status, metrics, quality, and a reason code.
Requested tasks can be pending, checking, positive, negative, insufficient, or
skipped. A negative result requires completed, qualified acquisition. Technical
failure does not itself imply either a clinical abnormality or its absence.
Explicit symptom reports carry their own onset metadata. Automatic eye geometry
is research-only; the separate visual-symptom questionnaire supplies the
eligible E evidence.

The interface presents acquisition progress and the current report, while
historical reports retain their original evidence. Photos in the following
section illustrate the acquisition setup and live feedback.
