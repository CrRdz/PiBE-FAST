\section{Multimodal Fusion}
\label{sec:fusion}
\textit{Decision-level fusion.}
The deployed pathway combines eligible component outcomes, independent symptom
reports, and onset metadata into urgency and completeness outputs.
Passive B/S triggers request guided acquisition; they are distinct from this
report assessment. Let $M_t$ contain current measurements and $S_t$ active
symptom events. Urgency is \textit{urgent} when any eligible positive has new
or unknown onset, \textit{warning} when all positives are explicitly longstanding,
and \textit{none} when no eligible positive exists.
Completeness independently requires a resolved outcome for every requested
component. Thus a new symptom can remain urgent while another task is incomplete.
A negative screen requires complete requested evidence without eligible positives.

E supplies questionnaire evidence; camera eye geometry is research-only.
B standing exemption applies only to an unstarted task with a reported balance
problem. Retries replace measurements without erasing independent symptoms.
Corrections require an event identifier and reason, preserving original onset
and history; an ordinary negative answer does not retract an earlier symptom.
After the last B/E symptom is corrected, its questionnaire awaits a new answer.
Asynchronous speech results must match the current recording attempt.
Reports retain immutable revisions; configured SQLite history persists positive,
reported-functional-problem, and correction reports. These rules are checked
separately from sensor accuracy in Section~\ref{sec:experiment_results}.

\textit{Research feature fusion.}
A separate 48-dimensional representation concatenates 19 measurements, five
quality values, five component masks, and 19 feature masks:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf z=[\mathbf x_B,\ldots,\mathbf x_S,
q_B,\ldots,q_S,r_B,\ldots,r_S,h_1,\ldots,h_{19}].
\end{equation}
B/E/F/A/S contribute 2/6/3/4/4 measurements. Unavailable features are zeroed
with masks set to one, distinguishing missingness from measured zero;
original metrics remain in audit records. T supplies onset metadata rather
than a sensor feature. This research vector and its severity summaries do not
produce the evaluated decisions. The present integration is rule-based evidence
fusion, with no demonstrated predictive gain from the research representation.
