\begin{abstract}
Home-based stroke warning-sign assessment requires responsiveness to abnormal signs with limited disruption during ordinary activity. PiBE-FAST integrates audiovisual perception, guided BE-FAST checks, and rule-based evidence fusion. It preserves reported symptoms across incomplete acquisition, retries, and explicit corrections. Software tests verify these interactions, while participant records characterize target response and nuisance triggering: 35 of 38 scripted targets had recorded triggers, with 0.480 false triggers per reference-negative online hour. These historical records are not linked to current runtime decision paths. The prototype supports evaluation of the monitoring-to-check workflow; reliable stroke warning and reduced final-alert burden remain to be established.
\end{abstract}
